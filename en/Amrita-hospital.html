About the Clinic

Inaugurated in August 2022, Amrita Hospital, Faridabad is India's largest private hospital, part of the globally recognized Mata Amritanandamayi Math network. This hospital has been a beacon of medical excellence since its inception.

The clinic is focused on providing patients with holistic and world-class medical care. Strategically located in Sector 88, this premier institution is easily accessible from the Delhi NCR region.

Advanced Infrastructure

  • World-class medical campus: Over 130 acres, with a built-up area of 1 million square meters.
  • Specialized centers: Comprehensive Cancer Center, organ transplant department (kidney, liver, and heart).
  • Latest technologies: Advanced radiology services equipped with PET CT, 3T MRI, and other modern imaging systems.
  • Robotic surgery: Minimally invasive robotic systems.
  • Artificial intelligence integration: AI and Machine Learning for accurate diagnosis and personalized treatment plans.
  • Eco-friendly design: Solar power systems and water recycling plants.
